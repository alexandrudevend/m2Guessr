Metin2 Guessr

Think you know the world of Metin2? Put your knowledge to the test with Metin2 Guessr!

In this game, you'll be shown a random screenshot from Metin2 and your goal is to guess the exact location where the picture was taken.

Recognize familiar maps, towns, landscapes, dungeons and hidden corners, and see how well you really know the world of Metin2.

  • Guess the location
  • Explore iconic Metin2 maps
  • Earn points based on your accuracy
  • Challenge yourself!

Whether you're a veteran player or simply enjoy exploring Metin2, Metin2 Guessr is a fun way to test your memory, observation skills and knowledge of the game.
